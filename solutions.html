Emergency SOP

The 'Golden Hour' 4-Step Incident Protocol

Acting within the first 120 minutes of financial fraud gives cyber police the highest chance of freezing the fraudulent beneficiary account.

1
Immediate 24/7 Action

Dial 1930 Immediately

Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930). Keep transaction details ready: UTR / Reference number, sender account, receiver bank name, and time of transaction.

📞 Call 1930 Now
2
Prevent Secondary Loss

Freeze Bank Accounts & Cards

Use your bank's mobile app to instantly lock debit/credit cards, disable netbanking, and toggle off international and online transactions.

3
Crucial for Police FIR

Preserve All Evidence

Do not delete chats, SMS, call logs, or URLs. Take clear screenshots of the fraudster's profile, UPI IDs, payment slips, and transaction IDs.

4
Legal Documentation

File Formal Complaint on Portal

Visit cybercrime.gov.in and lodge a comprehensive complaint. Obtain your National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP) Acknowledgement Number.
