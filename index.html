Understanding the Ecosystem

The Problem vs. The Community Solution

Cybercriminals leverage psychological manipulation (fear, urgency, greed). We fight back with awareness, verification, and rapid action.

⚠️

The Problem: How Fraud Happens

  • 🚨 Fear & Coercion: Scammers pose as police or CBI over video calls ('Digital Arrest').
  • 📱 Fake Links & APKs: Urgent SMS claiming power cut or bank KYC disconnection.
  • 💸 Greed Traps: Telegram work-from-home tasks and fake high-return crypto trading.
  • 🔄 UPI Confusion: Sending a QR code or PIN request claiming 'Scan to receive money'.
Explore Common Threats →
🛡️

The Solution: Community Defense

  • ⏱️ Golden Hour Protocol: Dial 1930 within 2 hours to freeze inter-bank money transfers.
  • ⚖️ Zero Panic Rule: Real Indian law enforcement never conducts online video arrests.
  • 🔍 Source Verification: Never install remote tools like AnyDesk, RustDesk, or TeamViewer for strangers.
  • 📡 Chakshu & Sanchar Saathi: Proactively report fraud numbers to get their SIMs disconnected.
